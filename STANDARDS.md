# Which standards apply? / Jakie standardy obowiązują?

Short answer: **the North Carolina Standard Course of Study for Biology** — the statewide standards. Wake County does not have its own separate biology standards.

Krótka odpowiedź: **stanowa podstawa programowa Karoliny Północnej (NC Standard Course of Study) dla biologii**. Hrabstwo Wake nie ma własnych, odrębnych standardów z biologii.

## The details

- **The standards are statewide.** North Carolina adopted a new Standard Course of Study for Science in **July 2023**; the new Biology standards reached classrooms in **2024–25**, the same year the redesigned state End-of-Course (EOC) Biology test first counted. Wake County Public Schools (WCPSS) teaches these state standards — a district can add pacing and materials, but not different standards.
- **The strands.** The 2023 Biology standards are organized around the major life-science strands: structures and processes of organisms (molecules to organisms), ecosystems and energy dynamics, heredity, and biological evolution. The seven course units in this library walk through exactly that territory: chemistry of life → cells → cellular processes → DNA and cell division → genetics and biotechnology → evolution → ecology.
- **"Honors" is depth, not different content.** An Honors Biology section covers the same NC standards as standard Biology, at greater depth and pace, per the state's honors-level course framework. Both take the same EOC.
- **The EOC matters.** Biology is one of North Carolina's EOC-tested courses — the state exam at the end of the year is written against these standards. The two-column (English | Polski) half of every page in this library is calibrated to that level.
- **AP Biology is a different, national framework.** The **AP Biology deep dive** section at the bottom of each page follows the College Board's AP Biology Course and Exam Description (CED) — eight units that map loosely onto this course's seven (Unit 1 Chemistry of Life, Unit 2 Cell Structure and Function, Unit 3 Cellular Energetics, Unit 4 Cell Communication and Cell Cycle, Unit 5 Heredity, Unit 6 Gene Expression and Regulation, Unit 7 Natural Selection, Unit 8 Ecology). That content is beyond what the NC course requires — it is there for enrichment and as a head start if AP Biology comes later.
