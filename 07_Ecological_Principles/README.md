# Unit 7 — Ecological Principles / Podstawy ekologii

[← All units / Wszystkie działy](../README.md)

| English | Polski |
|---|---|
| Ecology is the study of who lives with whom — and on whom. This unit follows the energy: from **producers** through **consumers** to **decomposers**, up the **trophic levels** where 90% is lost at every step. Then it watches **populations** grow — the explosive J-curve, the braked S-curve, and the **carrying capacity** where **limiting factors** call the stop. Next come the ways species live together: predator and prey, and the symbiosis family from win–win to freeloading to theft. It ends with the biggest consumer of all: humans — acid rain, dead zones, invasive species, the ozone story, and the carbon arithmetic of climate change. | Ekologia bada, kto z kim żyje — i czyim kosztem. Ten dział podąża za energią: od **producentów** przez **konsumentów** po **destruentów**, w górę **poziomów troficznych**, gdzie na każdym szczeblu przepada 90%. Potem patrzy, jak rosną **populacje** — wybuchowa krzywa J, przyhamowana krzywa S i **pojemność środowiska**, przy której stop ogłaszają **czynniki ograniczające**. Dalej sposoby współżycia gatunków: drapieżnik i ofiara oraz rodzina symbioz — od obopólnej korzyści przez jazdę na gapę po kradzież. Na końcu największy konsument ze wszystkich: człowiek — kwaśne deszcze, martwe strefy, gatunki inwazyjne, historia ozonu i węglowa arytmetyka zmiany klimatu. |
| **The storyline:** energy flows one way (matter cycles) → populations grow until something stops them → species interactions knit populations into communities → and one species, ours, now bends every one of those rules at planetary scale. | **Oś działu:** energia płynie w jedną stronę (materia krąży) → populacje rosną, aż coś je zatrzyma → oddziaływania międzygatunkowe splatają populacje w biocenozy → a jeden gatunek, nasz, nagina dziś każdą z tych reguł w skali planety. |

## The terms / Hasła

| Term | Po polsku | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| [Acid Rain](acid_rain/README.md) | kwaśne deszcze | SO₂ and NOₓ from burning fuels fall back as acid, killing lakes and forests. |
| [Algal Bloom](algal_bloom/README.md) | zakwit glonów | A nutrient-fed population explosion of algae that ends by suffocating the water. |
| [Bioaccumulation](bioaccumulation/README.md) | bioakumulacja | A pollutant piling up in one organism over its lifetime. |
| [Biological Magnification](biological_magnification/README.md) | biomagnifikacja | Toxin concentration multiplying at each step up the food chain. |
| [Biomass](biomass/README.md) | biomasa | The total dry mass of living material in an area or trophic level. |
| [Carbon Sink](carbon_sink/README.md) | pochłaniacz węgla | Anything that absorbs more carbon than it releases — forests, peat, the ocean. |
| [Carrying Capacity (K)](carrying_capacity/README.md) | pojemność środowiska (K) | The largest population an environment can support long-term. |
| [Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)](chlorofluorocarbons/README.md) | freony (CFC) | The refrigerant gases that ate the ozone layer — and were banned in time. |
| [Climate Change](climate_change/README.md) | zmiana klimatu | The human-driven warming of Earth's long-term averages. |
| [Commensalism](commensalism/README.md) | komensalizm | One species benefits, the other neither gains nor loses (+/0). |
| [Community](community/README.md) | biocenoza | All the interacting populations of different species in one area. |
| [Consumer](consumer/README.md) | konsument | An organism that gets energy by eating others. |
| [Decomposer](decomposer/README.md) | destruent (reducent) | Bacteria and fungi that break the dead back down to minerals. |
| [Eutrophication](eutrophication/README.md) | eutrofizacja | Over-fertilized water: bloom, die-off, oxygen crash, dead zone. |
| [Exponential Growth (J-curve)](exponential_growth/README.md) | wzrost wykładniczy (krzywa J) | Growth that speeds up as the population grows — doubling without limits. |
| [Fossil Fuels](fossil_fuels/README.md) | paliwa kopalne | Coal, oil, and gas — ancient buried carbon we burn in decades. |
| [Greenhouse Gases](greenhouse_gases/README.md) | gazy cieplarniane | Gases that trap Earth's outgoing heat — CO₂, methane, N₂O, and friends. |
| [Invasive Species](invasive_species/README.md) | gatunek inwazyjny | A non-native that spreads aggressively and causes harm. |
| [Limiting Factor](limiting_factor/README.md) | czynnik ograniczający | The scarcest resource or condition — the one that caps growth. |
| [Logistic Growth (S-curve)](logistic_growth/README.md) | wzrost logistyczny (krzywa S) | Growth that slows as resources tighten and levels off at K. |
| [Mitigation](mitigation/README.md) | łagodzenie zmian klimatu (mitygacja) | Cutting emissions and protecting sinks — attacking climate change at its cause. |
| [Mutualism](mutualism/README.md) | mutualizm | A relationship where both species benefit (+/+). |
| [Niche](niche/README.md) | nisza ekologiczna | A species' full way of making a living — its role, not its address. |
| [Nitrogen Fixation](nitrogen_fixation/README.md) | wiązanie azotu | Bacteria turning inert N₂ gas into nitrogen life can use. |
| [Non-Native Species](non_native_species/README.md) | gatunek obcy | A species humans moved outside its natural range — harmful or not. |
| [Parasitism](parasitism/README.md) | pasożytnictwo | One species feeds on a living host at its expense (+/−). |
| [Population](population/README.md) | populacja | All individuals of one species in one area at one time. |
| [Predator](predator/README.md) | drapieżnik | The hunter that catches, kills, and eats its prey. |
| [Prey](prey/README.md) | ofiara | The hunted — armed with camouflage, mimicry, and safety in numbers. |
| [Producer](producer/README.md) | producent | An organism that makes its own food — the base of every food chain. |
| [Symbiosis](symbiosis/README.md) | symbioza | Close, long-term living-together of different species — the umbrella term. |
| [Trophic Levels](trophic_levels/README.md) | poziomy troficzne | The feeding floors of an ecosystem, each passing on only ~10% of its energy. |
